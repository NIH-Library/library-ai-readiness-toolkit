# Verification Checklist

Use this checklist before relying on AI-assisted work, especially when the output includes claims, citations, summaries, statistics, recommendations, code, or language that may be reused with others.

| Check | Confirm before relying on the output |
|---|---|
| Claims | Are the claims supported by source text or reliable evidence? |
| Numbers | Are sample sizes, dates, units, thresholds, and statistics accurate? |
| Citations | Do the citations exist, and do they support the claim? |
| Assumptions | What did the model infer, assume, or fill in? |
| Scope | Is the output generalizing beyond the evidence? |
| Local context | Are there sensitive information, policy, confidentiality, copyright, licensing, privacy, or security risks? |
| Audience | Is the output appropriate for the intended audience? |
| Use case | Is the output being used for brainstorming, drafting, instruction, research, publication, or decision-making? |
| Accountability | Who will review, edit, approve, or take responsibility for the final use? |

## Staff reminder

AI output should be treated as unverified until checked. The more public, official, sensitive, or high-stakes the use, the more careful the verification process should be.
