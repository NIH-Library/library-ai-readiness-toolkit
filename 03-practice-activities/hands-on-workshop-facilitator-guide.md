# Hands-On Workshop Facilitator Guide

This workshop helps staff move from discussion to practice by using approved or appropriate generative AI tools, refining prompts, reviewing outputs, adding guardrails, and verifying AI-assisted work.

## Suggested length

60 to 75 minutes

## Workshop goals

Participants will:

- Practice using generative AI tools in low-risk scenarios.
- Refine prompts through iteration.
- Add guardrails to improve transparency and reduce common failure modes.
- Apply verification techniques before relying on AI-assisted work.
- Discuss how these practices apply to library and user-support contexts.

## Suggested agenda

- 5 minutes: Introduce workshop goals
- 10 minutes: Review responsible-use reminders
- 20 minutes: Activity 1, explore tools and iterate
- 25 minutes: Activity 2, prompt engineering for accuracy and verification
- 10 minutes: Group discussion and takeaways

## Facilitator preparation

Before the workshop:

- Identify locally approved or appropriate AI tools.
- Choose low-risk examples.
- Avoid sensitive, private, confidential, unpublished, or restricted content.
- Decide whether participants will use live tools or provided sample outputs.
- Prepare reminders about local policies, data restrictions, copyright, licensing, and verification expectations.

## Core message

Prompting can improve usefulness, but verification determines whether the output is trustworthy enough to use.
