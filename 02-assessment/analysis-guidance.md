# Analysis Guidance

This file provides basic guidance for analyzing pre- and post-training self-assessment data.

## What to calculate

For matched confidence items:

- Pre-training mean
- Post-training mean
- Mean change
- Percentage selecting "very confident" or "extremely confident"
- Items with the largest gains
- Items with the lowest post-training confidence

For post-only outcome items:

- Percentage agreeing or strongly agreeing
- Areas with the strongest perceived impact
- Areas needing additional support

For open-ended responses:

- Recurring themes
- Common questions
- Future training needs
- Examples of desired support

## Important interpretation notes

If responses are anonymous and cannot be matched by individual respondent, describe results as aggregate shifts rather than individual learning gains.

Use careful language such as:

> Average self-reported confidence increased across the matched items.

Avoid language such as:

> Participants improved by a specific amount.

## Suggested reporting language

Use:

> Aggregate results suggest increased self-reported confidence and perceived readiness after the training series.

Avoid:

> The training proved that staff learned these skills.

## Limitations to report

- Anonymous or unpaired responses
- Self-reported confidence rather than observed behavior
- Small cohort size, if applicable
- Single-institution context, if applicable
- No long-term follow-up, if applicable
- Rapidly changing AI tools and policies
