# Post-Training Assessment

This file provides adaptable survey items for assessing staff confidence, perceived preparedness, and future support needs after generative AI training.

## Suggested purpose

Use this survey after training to assess aggregate changes in staff confidence, perceived training outcomes, and remaining support needs.

## Suggested scale

1 = Not at all confident  
2 = Slightly confident  
3 = Moderately confident  
4 = Very confident  
5 = Extremely confident

## Repeated confidence items

Repeat the same confidence items used in the pre-training survey so that aggregate pre/post comparison is possible.

## Post-training outcome items

Ask respondents to indicate their level of agreement with statements such as:

1. The training increased my understanding of generative AI.
2. The training increased my understanding of responsible AI use.
3. The training helped me recognize concerns related to accuracy, privacy, copyright, licensing, or policy.
4. The training increased my understanding of how to verify AI-generated outputs.
5. The training helped me feel more prepared to respond to AI-related questions.
6. The training provided useful examples for my work.
7. The training helped me identify when to answer, verify, pause, or refer AI-related questions.

## Open-ended questions

1. What was the most useful part of the training?
2. What AI-related topics would you like more support with?
3. What questions do you still have?
4. What additional examples, scenarios, or resources would help you apply this training?
