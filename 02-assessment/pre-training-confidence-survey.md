# Pre-Training Confidence Survey

This file provides adaptable survey items for assessing staff confidence before generative AI training.

## Suggested purpose

Use this survey before training to identify staff confidence, common AI-related questions, and areas where staff want additional support.

## Suggested scale

1 = Not at all confident  
2 = Slightly confident  
3 = Moderately confident  
4 = Very confident  
5 = Extremely confident

## Sample confidence items

How confident are you in your ability to:

1. Explain what generative AI is in plain language.
2. Explain common strengths and limitations of generative AI tools.
3. Recognize when AI output may require verification.
4. Identify responsible-use concerns related to privacy, copyright, licensing, policy, or accuracy.
5. Discuss possible uses of AI in research, teaching, or library service contexts.
6. Answer basic user questions about generative AI.
7. Ask follow-up questions before recommending an AI workflow.
8. Know when to refer an AI-related question to another person, office, or policy source.
9. Engage with users who have questions about AI.

## Open-ended questions

1. What AI-related questions are you currently hearing from users, colleagues, or partners?
2. What AI-related questions do you feel least prepared to answer?
3. What topics would make you feel more prepared to respond to AI-related questions?
4. Are there local policies, tools, or workflows you want clarified during training?
5. What would you like to be able to do by the end of this training?
