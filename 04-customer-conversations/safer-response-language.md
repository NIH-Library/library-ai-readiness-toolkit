# Safer Response Language for AI Questions

These phrases can help staff respond to AI-related questions in a calm, consistent, and role-appropriate way.

## Clarifying the task

- Before we choose a tool, let's clarify what information would go into it.
- That may be appropriate, but the tool, data, and intended use matter.
- Let's start with what you are trying to accomplish.

## Naming risk

- I would pause before uploading that.
- This may involve sensitive, internal, copyrighted, licensed, or restricted information.
- AI output should be treated as unverified until checked.
- The question may depend on local policy, tool terms, or another authority.

## Offering safer next steps

- A safer approach may be to work from public information, your own notes, or a lower-risk excerpt.
- We can identify what needs to be verified before relying on the output.
- We can look for the relevant local guidance before moving forward.

## Maintaining role boundaries

- This is outside what I can approve, but I can help identify the right next step.
- This may need review by the office or person responsible for that policy.
- I cannot make a legal, privacy, security, or compliance determination, but I can help clarify the issue.
