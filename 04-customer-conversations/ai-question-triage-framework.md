# AI Question Triage Framework

Use this framework when responding to AI-related questions from users, colleagues, or partners.

The goal is not to memorize every rule. The goal is to identify the type of question being asked and choose a safer next step.

## Answer

Answer when:

- The question is low risk.
- The guidance is clear.
- The question fits the library's role.
- The user mainly needs help thinking through the task, tool, or verification process.

## Verify

Verify when the question involves:

- AI-generated citations
- Statistics or numbers
- Summaries
- Claims about research or evidence
- Code or analysis
- Any output that will be reused, published, submitted, or shared

## Pause

Pause when the question involves:

- Sensitive data
- Identifiable information
- Internal or non-public information
- Copyrighted or licensed content
- Unpublished work
- High-stakes decisions
- Official communications
- Unclear tool use
- Unclear data handling

## Refer

Refer when the question depends on:

- Institutional policy
- Privacy, security, or compliance review
- Legal or copyright interpretation
- Journal, funder, or publisher requirements
- Public communications clearance
- Formal approval or exception-making
- Another office or authority

## Quick decision questions

Before answering, ask:

1. What is the task?
2. What information will go into the tool?
3. Which tool will be used?
4. How will the output be used?
5. Who will see the final product?
6. What needs to be verified, approved, or referred?
