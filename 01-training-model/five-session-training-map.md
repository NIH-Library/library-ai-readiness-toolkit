# Five-Session Training Map

This file will describe the five-part training sequence used to build library staff readiness for generative AI questions.

## Sessions

1. AI fundamentals
2. AI in research and library services
3. Responsible AI use
4. Hands-on prompting and verification
5. Discussing AI with customers

## Adaptation notes

Adapt the sequence, examples, policies, tools, and scenarios to your local library context.
